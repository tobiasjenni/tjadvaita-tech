01 / The pathअभ्यास · abhyāsa

More than a posture.
A way of being.

Julia’s teaching begins with the body and opens into the whole of life. Her practice draws on classical yoga, Sanskrit study, Qigong and meditation, shaped by years of living and teaching in Nepal and India.

Each session invites a quieter kind of attention: alignment without force, breath without hurry, and space to notice what is already present. You can arrive with experience or begin exactly where you are.

The teacher

Julia

For more than fifteen years, Julia has devoted herself to practices of conscious movement and inner inquiry. Her path has taken her through Sanskrit study, yoga and Qigong, and into years of living and leading retreats in the Himalayan region of Nepal.

Her classes bring that lived experience into an attentive, grounded space for your own exploration.
